# FPS Optimizer Pro Mod
**By taumk**

See your frame rate at a glance and squeeze more FPS out of heavy levels, all from one lightweight mod.

## FPS Counter
- Pinned to the **top-left corner** and visible in menus, levels and the editor
- **Color-coded**: green when you hit your target FPS, yellow when slightly below, red when low
- Optional **frame time (ms)** readout
- Adjustable **scale**, **opacity** and **refresh rate**
- Option to show it **only inside levels**

## Optimizer
- **Particle limiter**: choose what percentage of particles can spawn while you are in a level (100% = vanilla, 0% = none). Menus are unaffected. Defaults to 50%.
- **Hide decorations** *(experimental, off by default)*: hides decoration-type objects while playing for a big FPS boost on heavy levels. Levels will look plainer while it is on.

## Settings
Everything is configurable in the mod settings: counter on/off, in-level only, frame time, color coding, scale, opacity, update interval, particle density and decoration hiding.

## Notes
- Safe to toggle at any time. Settings apply instantly.
- If you hit a bug, include your Geode and Geometry Dash versions when reporting it.
